Use this when a task must land in one reply with no clarifying round trip.
Covers: how to state assumptions, scope the deliverable and show evidence.
Sources: plan.md (agent-library), front 1.

## One-shot pattern
- Pick the most likely reading of the request and say so under Assumptions.
- Do the whole task, verify it, then reply with result + evidence.
- If a step is impossible here (no network, no GPU), mark the affected output `untested` and say why.
